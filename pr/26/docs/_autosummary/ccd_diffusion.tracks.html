tracks#

Measure the charge diffusion kernel of the IRIS CCDs from glancing particle tracks.

The tracks are stored in data/iris_tracks.npz and data/iris_tracks.csv, and the result of fitting the diffusion model to every track is stored in data/iris_fits.csv. Fitting every track takes several minutes, so the fits are only recomputed by running python -m ccd_diffusion.tracks.

flat(chip)

The fits of the flat tracks on the given CCD.

stack(chip[, num_offset])

Stack the flat tracks on the given CCD, placing every slice at its fractional depth and aligning it on its fitted centerline, with each pixel's charge spread uniformly over its own width.

widths(chip)

Measure the diffusion width in each depth bin without a parametric model, by summing the misfit of every slice of every flat track on the given CCD over a grid of trial widths and minimizing in each bin.

pooled(chip, scans[, iterations])

Choose the \(\sigma_d\) of a CCD by summing the misfits of its flat tracks over the grid of \(\sigma_d\) and taking the minimum.

save_depleted(depleted)

Write the pooled fits to data/iris_depleted.csv, where depleted() will find them.

depleted(chip)

Load the pooled fit of \(\sigma_d\) on the given CCD from data/iris_depleted.csv.

load([directory])

Load the particle tracks extracted from the IRIS level-1 images.

frames([directory])

Load the list of IRIS level-1 frames searched for tracks.

width(depth, critical_depth, width_max[, ...])

The standard deviation of the charge cloud, in pixels, for charge deposited at the given fractional depth.

fractions(position, width, slope)

The fraction of each slice's charge expected in each of the \(2 h + 1\) pixels across the track.

loss(track, position, width)

A robust misfit between the observed and modeled charge fractions, \(\sum \ln(1 + r^2 / 2)\), where \(r\) is the residual in units of the read noise.

scan(track)

Fit the diffusion model to a single track at every \(\sigma_d\).

fit(track[, width_depleted])

Fit the diffusion model to a single track at a given \(\sigma_d\).

fit_all(tracks)

Fit the diffusion model to every given track, using one thread per CPU, choosing one \(\sigma_d\) per CCD by pooling the misfits of its flat tracks (see ccd_diffusion.tracks.pooled()).

fits()

Load the result of fit_all() for every track returned by load() from data/iris_fits.csv.

save(fits, depleted)

Write the given fits to data/iris_fits.csv, where fits() will find them, and the pooled fits of \(\sigma_d\) to data/iris_depleted.csv, where ccd_diffusion.tracks.depleted() will.

same_pixel(fit)

The measured probability that two charges from the same slice are collected in the same pixel column, \(\sum_j f_j^2\), corrected for the read-noise bias \(\sum_j \epsilon_j^2\), and NaN for a slice too faint to be Track.usable.

same_pixel_model(fit, critical_depth, width_max)

The probability that two charges from the same slice are collected in the same pixel column predicted by the diffusion model for the best-fit centerline of the given track.

profile(chip)

Bin the measured and modeled same-pixel probabilities of every flat track on the given CCD by depth.

summary(chip)

Summarize the diffusion measurement on the given CCD.
