Track browser
=============

Every track the finder extracted, as the package holds it: the seven-pixel
cutout, re-centered on the integer part of the fitted centerline in every
slice and shown with the back surface at the left, and the fit of
Equation 1 of the article to it. The filters follow the selection of the
article: a *flat* track is tight (the fit constrains :math:`t_c` to within
0.15 and improves on no diffusion by ten units of misfit), has no Bragg
rise along its length, and has a depleted end (:math:`t_c \le 0.7`); the
*core* tracks are the flat tracks with :math:`0.25 < t_c < 0.6`.

Click a cutout to see it beside the model at its fit, its parameters with
their intervals, its charge per slice, and the same-pixel probability of
each slice against the model. The model is evaluated in the page from the
fit parameters, so that it can be checked against the cutout by eye. A
track's link in the :doc:`frame gallery <frames>` opens it here, and the
page's address remembers the track shown.
