Frame gallery
=============

One level-1 image from each camera of every campaign, the one in which
the finder kept the most tracks, fetched from the archive when the
documentation is built and rendered as the example images of the article
are: cropped to the part of the frame that was read out, with the inverse
hyperbolic sine stretch above the background saturating at 400 data
numbers, unread pixels white, and the tracks the finder kept outlined in
red. Most of the hits are the short streaks and dots; the tracks are the
long, thin ones nearly aligned with the columns. Each track's name opens
it in the :doc:`track browser <browser>`.
